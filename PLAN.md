## 📌 Project Overview

PlotWeaver is an AI-powered visual storytelling workspace where writers build stories using connected nodes instead of long text documents.

The AI acts as a Creative Partner, helping writers by:

- ✅ Detecting plot holes
- ✅ Maintaining plot continuity
- ✅ Tracking character arcs
- ✅ Checking timeline consistency
- ✅ Enforcing world-building rules
- ✅ Tracking relationships
- ✅ Suggesting foreshadowing
- ✅ Finding unresolved plot threads
- ✅ Improving story pacing
- ✅ Brainstorming alternative story directions

> **Important**: The AI does not write the story. It assists the writer in creating a better one.

## 🎯 MVP (What we'll build in 10 Days)

Instead of building every feature, we'll focus on:

- ✅ **Interactive Story Canvas**: Create and connect story nodes visually.
- ✅ **AI Continuity Checker**: Detect contradictions and plot holes.
- ✅ **World Rule Checker**: Ensure story events follow user-defined rules.
- ✅ **AI Brainstorm Assistant**: Suggest creative ways to fix inconsistencies.
- ✅ **Export Story Outline**: Convert the visual graph into a structured outline.
